Shul Rav

3 Photo 3: Rabbi Photo images/3.jpg
Rabbi Simantov Yanetz
Spiritual Leader

Rabbi Simantov Yanetz was born in Israel in 1985. After completing high school, he furthered his education at ITRI Yeshiva in Jerusalem, earning his Rabbinical degree (Semicha) from the esteemed Rosh Yeshiva of ITRI, Dayan Rabbi Shlomo Fisher. He continued his studies at Torat Moshe Kollel in Jerusalem under the guidance of the former Chief Rabbi of Israel, Rabbi Shlomo Amar, where he received an additional Rabbinical degree.

In 2012, Rabbi Yanetz relocated to New York and served as a Rabbi at Magen David Yeshiva in Brooklyn. By 2016, he earned his Semicha from Rabbi Eliyahu Ben Chaim, the Av Bet Din of Queens, and was sought out by the BJCB community to lead our shul. Over the past decade, the community has flourished under his leadership, developing numerous programs and welcoming new families.

Torah Learning & Community Programs

Discover our active schedule of learning, youth programs, and community life.

Featured Program

🌟 "Super Tuesday" Night Chavruta & Shiurim

Our flagship weekly evening of energized Torah study! Join community members of all levels every Tuesday night for an electric Beit Midrash atmosphere. Features dedicated one-on-one Chavruta learning, warm dinner and refreshments, and engaging classes in Gemara, Halacha, and Zera Shimshon led by Rabbi Simantov Yanetz. Everyone is welcome to grab a sefer, find a partner, and learn.

📚 Weekly Sunday Gemara Class

An in-depth Gemara series diving deep into the sugyot with Rabbi Simantov Yanetz. A warm, welcoming group celebrating regular collective Siyumim together upon completing masechtot.

🕯️ Shabbat Learning Program

Winter: Weekly Friday Night Learning Program filled with uplifting Torah and spiritual preparation for the week.
Summer: Dedicated Shabbat afternoon / day learning sessions held in the sanctuary prior to Mincha.

🧸 Shabbat Morning Children's Program

Every Saturday morning during Shabbat Shacharit and Musaf services! Held in a separate youth room with supervised, age-appropriate activities, davening, engaging Parsha stories, games, and treats.

👨‍👦 Father & Children (אבות ובנים)

A cherished community tradition bringing fathers, sons, and daughters together for quality Torah study, exciting raffles, prizes, and delicious snacks and pizza.

🌸 Women's Events & Challah Baking

Engaging, dedicated programming for women in our community, including special Challah Baking evenings, inspirational holiday shiurim, and creative Women's Sip & Paint nights.

🎉 Holiday Celebrations & Siyumim

Vibrant communal gatherings throughout the Jewish year: Simchat Torah hakafot, Chanukah parties, Purim seudot, the annual Lag Ba'Omer bonfire, and all-night Shavuot learning.

Housed Inside BJCB

📖 Yeshiva Etzion

We are proud to host Yeshiva Etzion within our sanctuary building. Founded in 2003 under the leadership of Rosh Yeshiva Rabbi Avraham Gaon, Yeshiva Etzion provides a warm, dedicated environment for young men and adults to learn Torah without financial burden. Offering structured Morning and Night Seder programs (Sunday through Thursday at 9:30 AM and 8:00 PM), open halachic discussions, and college credit opportunities.
